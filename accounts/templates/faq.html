Starter Packs

What is a starter pack?

A starter pack is a collection of accounts that you can share with others. It can be used to help new users find accounts to follow, or to promote specific communities or topics.

How to create a starter pack?

Login with your Mastodon account and go to Create starter pack page.

How to get added to a starter pack?

If you want to be added to a starter pack, you can reach out to the creator of the starter pack and ask them to add you. You can also create your own starter pack and add yourself to it.

How to see starter packs I am in?

You can see all starter packs you are in by navigating to Starter packs containing you page. You do not need to have a fedidevs account to see starter packs you are in.

How to get removed from a starter pack?

If you want to be removed from a starter packs reach out through email or on Mastodon.

Which accounts can I add to a starter pack?

Any Mastodon account or an account from a Mastodon-compatible instance can be added to a starter pack as long as it has expressed consent to be listed by being set as be discoverable and not opt-out of search engine indexing.

Accounts on GoToSocial and Misskey-family instances (Sharkey, Firefish, Iceshrimp, Catodon, Akkoma) can be added as well — see Other Fediverse software for the settings that control it there.

To make sure your account is discoverable and opt-in to search engine indexing, go to Preferences - Privacy and reach tab ({your_instance}/settings/privacy) and check the following checkboxes:

  1. Feature profile and posts in discovery algorithms
  2. Include profile page in search engines

Developer listings

How to get listed as a developer?

There are a few prerequisites for your account to be listed on FediDevs.com

1. Your account must be discoverable and not opt-out of search engine indexing

Go to Preferences - Public profile - Privacy and reach tab ({your_instance}/settings/privacy) and check the following checkboxes:

  1. Feature profile and posts in discovery algorithms
  2. Include profile page in search engines

How often is the data refreshed?

The data is refreshed about once per day, but if you want your profile on FediDevs.com updated sooner, send me an email and I'll update it manually.

How to get your account removed from FediDevs.com?

The easiest way is to Opt-out of search engine indexing in your Mastodon Privacy and reach page. Uncheck the checkbox that says "Feature profile and posts in discovery algorithms" or "Include profile page in search engines" at {your_instance}/settings/privacy. This will remove your account on next sync (in about 1 day).

If you don't want to wait until the next sync please reach out through email and I'll remove you immediately.

How to get your instance removed from FediDevs.com?

If you are an admin of an instance that is listed above, but don't want me indexing it anymore, please send an email. Once I confirm your identity I will remove all accounts from your instance and never index your instance again.

Other Fediverse software

I'm on GoToSocial. How do I let people add me to starter packs?

Starter packs work with GoToSocial: you can log in with your GoToSocial account, create starter packs, and be added to them.

To let people add you, check Mark Your Account as Discoverable by Search Engines and Include it in the Instance Directory under Visibility and Privacy in your instance's settings panel. Without it your account is treated as not having consented to being listed and will show up as hidden in starter packs.

I'm on Misskey, Sharkey, Firefish, Iceshrimp, Catodon, or Akkoma. How do I let people add me to starter packs?

Starter packs work with these servers too: logging in, creating starter packs, and being added to them are all supported.

Unlike Mastodon, there is no "feature profile in discovery algorithms" checkbox to turn on, so people can add you to starter packs as long as you haven't enabled the privacy option that asks search engines and crawlers not to index your profile. If you have that opt-out enabled, your account will show up as hidden in starter packs; disable it and the change is picked up on the next sync (about a day). To be hidden or removed immediately, email me.
